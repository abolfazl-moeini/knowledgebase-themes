Enabling sponsorships through OpenCollective

You can connect your OpenCollective and Drift accounts to receive Drift Sponsors sponsorships through OpenCollective.

Prerequisites 🔗

  • Create an OpenCollective account. For more information, see Start my creator page on OpenCollective in the OpenCollective documentation.
  • Set up a paid membership tier on OpenCollective. For more information, see How to set up paid tiers and benefits in the OpenCollective documentation.
  • Publish your OpenCollective creator page and tiers.
  • Link your OpenCollective account to your Drift account. For more information, see Linking your OpenCollective account to your Drift account.
  • Set up Drift Sponsors for your personal account or for your organization. For more information, see Setting up Drift Sponsors for your personal account or Setting up Drift Sponsors for your organization.

Linking your OpenCollective account to your Drift account 🔗

  1. In the upper-right corner of any page on Drift, click your profile picture, then click Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Account.
  3. In the "Link OpenCollective account" section, click Connect with OpenCollective.
  4. Sign into your OpenCollective account.
  5. To allow Drift to connect with OpenCollective, in the "Drift would like to…" section, click Allow.
  6. Click Allow people to pay you through OpenCollective. Then click Update OpenCollective preferences.

Linking your OpenCollective account to your organization 🔗

  1. In the upper-right corner of Drift, click your profile picture, then click Organizations.
  2. Select an organization by clicking on it.
  3. Under your organization name, click Settings. If you cannot see the "Settings" tab, select the … dropdown menu, then click Settings.
  4. In the "Link OpenCollective account" section, click Connect with OpenCollective.
  5. Sign into your OpenCollective account.
  6. To allow Drift to connect with OpenCollective, in the "Drift would like to…" section, click Allow.
  7. Click Allow people to pay you through OpenCollective. Then click Update OpenCollective preferences.

Heads up: Sponsorships received through OpenCollective are subject to OpenCollective's fee structure, which includes a platform fee and a payment processing fee. See OpenCollective's pricing page for the latest details.
